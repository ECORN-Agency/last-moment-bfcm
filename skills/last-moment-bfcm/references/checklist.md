# Last Moment BFCM: 22 checks for your Shopify store

Black Friday is **27 November 2026**, Cyber Monday **30 November**. This is the final-week list: what to lock, test and
prepare now, with where to click in the Shopify admin.

**Where it comes from.** The checks follow Nik Sharma's
[last-minute BFCM prep checklist](https://www.nik.co/newsletter/2024-09-22-the-6-page-last-minute-bfcm-prep-checklist),
his [2025 BFCM playbook](https://www.nik.co/newsletter/2025-08-31-nik-sharmas-2025-bfcm-playbook) and his
[BFCM 2026 planning guide](https://www.nik.co/guides/bfcm-2026), summarised in our words. Each check says which one it
comes from. The Shopify admin paths, and the few checks marked **ECORN**, are ours. Read Nik's originals for the full
reasoning and examples.

Admin menu names are as of November 2026 and can differ slightly by plan or language.

## 1. The offer

- [ ] **1. Write the offer down in one place: what, which products, start and end time, reserved stock.**
      Everyone (site, ads, email, support, creators) works from that one note. *(Nik, 2026 guide)*
- [ ] **2. Give Black Friday and Cyber Monday their own offers** instead of one deal for four days, for example a bundle
      on Friday and a spend-more-save-more tier on Monday. *(Nik, 2024 checklist and 2026 guide)*
- [ ] **3. Make discounts automatic where you can, so nobody has to type a code.**
      *Discounts › Create discount › Method: Automatic discount.* *(Nik, 2024 checklist)*
- [ ] **4. Use auto-apply discount links in every ad, email and creator link.**
      *Discounts › (your code) › Promote › Get a shareable link*: the link applies the code by itself. *(Nik, 2024 and 2025)*
- [ ] **5. Cover your customers' time zones**: start a little early or end a little late so the far time zone gets the
      full sale. Check the store time zone in *Settings › General*. *(Nik, 2024 checklist)*
- [ ] **6. Test every code and every campaign link end to end, on a phone.** Nik's 2026 schedule puts this by
      20 November. *(Nik, 2025 playbook and 2026 guide)*

## 2. The storefront

- [ ] **7. Show the sale everywhere people land**: homepage hero, collection banners and the announcement bar, with the
      bar kept to one line on mobile. *Online Store › Themes › Customize.* *(Nik, 2024 checklist)*
- [ ] **8. Show strike-through prices on product pages.**
      *Products › Bulk edit › Compare-at price.* *(Nik, 2025 playbook)*
- [ ] **9. Use real urgency only**: a countdown to the actual end time, low-stock labels where stock is really low.
      *(Nik, 2025 playbook)*
- [ ] **10. Put the sale terms in the footer** (dates, exclusions, returns), and give support the same text.
      *(Nik, 2025 playbook)*
- [ ] **11. Turn the email pop-up into a Black Friday early-access sign-up.** *(Nik, 2024 checklist and 2025 playbook)*

## 3. Tech and tracking

- [ ] **12. Freeze site and app updates until the sale is over.** If something must change, test it on a copy first.
      *(Nik, 2024 checklist)* **ECORN:** before the freeze, duplicate the live theme
      (*Online Store › Themes › (···) › Duplicate*) so you can roll back in one click.
- [ ] **13. Remove heavy apps, unused pop-ups and old pixels for speed.**
      *Online Store › Themes › Customize › App embeds*, and *Apps* for apps you no longer use. *(Nik, 2025 playbook)*
- [ ] **14. Check that pixels fire, heatmaps record and a post-purchase survey is live.** *(Nik, 2024 checklist)*

## 4. Email and SMS

- [ ] **15. Load every email and SMS before the sale starts**: launch, "24 hours left", "last call".
      *(Nik, 2024 checklist)*
- [ ] **16. Set up sold-out and back-in-stock messages, with a back-in-stock sign-up on product pages.**
      *(Nik, 2024 checklist)*
- [ ] **17. Write two plain-text emails you hope not to send**: "technical difficulties" and "shipping delay".
      *(Nik, 2024 checklist)*

## 5. Support and fulfilment

- [ ] **18. Prepare a BFCM FAQ and saved replies**, including "can I combine this with another discount?".
      *(Nik, 2025 playbook)*
- [ ] **19. Pad delivery estimates and say so up front** if the warehouse will be slower. *(Nik, 2025 playbook)*
- [ ] **20. Name who watches the store during the sale.** *(Nik, 2026 guide)* **ECORN:** keep
      *Analytics › Live View* open on a second screen and bookmark [status.shopify.com](https://status.shopify.com)
      to see at once whether a problem is yours or the platform's.

## 6. Ads, creators and partners

- [ ] **21. Update your Meta and TikTok catalogs with sale prices, and leave time for ads stuck in review.**
      *(Nik, 2024 checklist)*
- [ ] **22. Brief creators and affiliates: dates, offers, gift with purchase, their code and link.** *(Nik, 2024 checklist)*

---

**Note for 2026:** Nik's 2024 checklist mentions Shopify Scripts for automatic discounts. Several 2026 guides report
that Shopify Scripts stopped running on 30 June 2026, so use automatic discounts or Shopify Functions instead.
